How a take works

  1. Pieces as they stand just before the move, as the board diagram shows them. Both arms to ready: the leader's motors switch on, both arms glide home, the leader is released again.
  2. When every joint is green, press ● Record on the move's row: the follower copies the leader from the ready pose.
  3. Make the move with the leader: down, close on the piece, carry, open, back up to the ready pose. For a capture, first lift the white piece off the destination square and drop it beside the board, then move the black piece. ■ Stop & save Space, or Discard Esc.
  4. Play back repeats a take from the ready pose. Re-record a move whenever the pieces around it change.
