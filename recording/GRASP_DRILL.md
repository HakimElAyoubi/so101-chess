## Why this drill
On the arm, most failures are the grasp skill closing beside the piece (27 of 34 misses closed more than 6 mm from it,
2026-10-28). Each drill episode starts **exactly where the player hands over to the skill** (both arms auto-move to the
player's grasp hover above the square), and the piece stands **off-centre on purpose** while the model's green dot
stays at the square's centre, as when playing: the model learns to grasp the piece wherever it stands in its square.

## Board
The eval setup: black in the start position; white rook g1, bishops f1 and c1, pawns c2, a3 and g3, king e1.
Keep a spare white pawn, knight, rook and queen from the box at hand for the tours.

## Parts (resumable: the recorder starts where it stopped)
| part | what | grasps |
|---|---|---|
| A | the 16 black pieces at home (crowded back ranks), 3 spots each | 48 |

| B | tours over the open board: each of the 12 kinds (6 types x 2 colours) visits 14 squares of ranks 1-6 | 168 |

## Each grasp (~30 s)
1. **Put the piece** on the square at the spot shown (the page draws the square with a ring where the piece goes: the
   centre, or ~6 mm toward a side or corner). By hand, or with the arm: between episodes the follower follows the leader.
2. **Enter** (Arm to the hover): both arms glide up and over to the hover above the square. Hands off the leader.
3. Check the live view: the **green dot** is what the model sees, the **white ring** where the piece should stand.
   Adjust the piece by hand if needed. Take the leader handle.
4. **Space** (Start): the leader is released, the follower follows it from the hover. **Grasp the piece, lift it ~3 cm,
   hold it still for a moment, Space** (Stop): saved.
5. Put the piece where the next instruction says (it is usually the next square of the same tour).

**R** discards a recording (put the piece back, Enter again). **S** skips an item. **Finish and upload** saves the
dataset and uploads it (private); you can stop after any part and resume another day.

## Tips
- Grasp the way you would in a game, one clean attempt. If you fumble, it's fine to retry within the same episode
  (the model learns recoveries too), but a knocked-over piece is a discard (R).
- Vary nothing else on purpose: the variety comes from the spots and squares.
- If the board gets moved, recalibrate it in the player (tour, C) before going on: the hovers use the latest board pose.
